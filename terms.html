Section 06Assumption of risk

Off-road driving on Moab's trails is inherently dangerous. Risks include, but are not limited to, vehicle rollover, collision, mechanical failure, falls, serious injury, permanent disability, and death, as well as damage to your property.

The trails are remote. Cell coverage is limited or absent in places, and emergency medical response can be significantly delayed. You may be far from a hospital.

You are choosing to take part voluntarily, and you accept these risks. You are responsible for your own medical, rescue, and evacuation costs. We strongly recommend carrying both medical insurance and travel insurance that covers off-road recreation.

These terms are not the waiver

A separate liability waiver and release must be signed before you take part. Dave will send it to you after you register. Signing it is required — you cannot drive or ride without it on file.

Section 07What you must complete before you arrive

  • Tell Dave in advance about food allergies, dietary restrictions, and any medical condition or medication that could matter in a remote setting.

We cannot let you drive or ride without the OHV card and the signed waiver. No refund is given if you arrive without them.
